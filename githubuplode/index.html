🚀 WHAT IS THIS WEBSITE FOR?

"Imagine waking up 200 million miles from home. Your solar wings are heavy with red dust. The Martian sun is setting... You have ONE final transmission to send to Earth before eternal silence..."

Since 1969, humanity has left over 200 metric tons of robotic rovers, landers, and Apollo descent stages across the Moon and Mars. Too often, textbooks dismiss them as cold "discarded space hardware" or lifeless debris. Echoes of the Orbit resurrects these silent relics as living, heroic legends. We transform authentic NASA Planetary Data System telemetry, high-resolution orbital imagery, and real final radio transmissions into an interactive, multi-generational storytelling experience.

🪐 1. 3D Globe Exploration
Spin high-precision 3D globes of Mars and the Moon. Locate 13 verified landing sites with authentic NASA HiRISE and LROC geodetic coordinates.
📖 2. 3-Tier Adaptive Stories
Content adapting dynamically to you: Kids get 3D comic flipbooks with robot read-alouds, Teens get Gen Z vibe checks, and Adults get deep scientific research dossiers.
🎬 3. 4K Reconstructions & Audio
Watch 4K continuous 3D camera fly-arounds reconstructed from NASA surface imagery, and hear the poignant final transmissions sent before perpetual shutdown.

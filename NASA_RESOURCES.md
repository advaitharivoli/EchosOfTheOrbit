## 2. NASA Solar System Treks (Trek.nasa.gov)
NASA's Solar System Treks are online suites of browser-based tools that allow for mapping, exploration, and 3D analysis of planetary surfaces from orbiter datasets:
* **Mars Trek:** [https://trek.nasa.gov/mars/](https://trek.nasa.gov/mars/) (Provides HiRISE, CTX, and MOLA topographic elevation maps for Mars).
* **Moon Trek:** [https://trek.nasa.gov/moon/](https://trek.nasa.gov/moon/) (Provides Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter (LRO) NAC imagery down to meter-scale resolution showing actual Apollo descent stage hardware and LRV wheel tracks).

In **Echoes of the Orbit**, clicking *"Explore Site on NASA Treks"* opens the exact coordinate coordinates on NASA Treks so students can view the actual satellite photography of where the hardware rests.

## 3. NASA 3D Resources & Meshy.ai Integration
* **Official NASA 3D Models Directory:** [https://science.nasa.gov/](https://science.nasa.gov/) and NASA 3D archives provide open-access 3D mesh files for Apollo, Curiosity, Perseverance, InSight, and MER rovers.
* **Meshy.ai AI 3D Generation:**
  For rovers and equipment where historical models are complex, students can use **Meshy.ai** to generate textured 3D models using:
  1. **Text-to-3D Prompting:** (Prompts are automatically provided in each equipment dossier, e.g., *"mars exploration rover opportunity robotic vehicle gold foil solar arrays"*).
  2. **Image-to-3D:** Uploading historical NASA mission photos into Meshy.ai to generate textured 3D assets.
  3. **Direct File Import:** Users can drag-and-drop or select any `.glb` or `.gltf` model directly into the 3D Holographic Chamber in the app!

## 4. Authentic Telemetry & Radio Transmission Logs
* **Opportunity (MER-B) Sol 5111 Downlink:** Atmospheric opacity (tau) exceeded 10.8 during the June 2018 global dust storm. JPL received the final telemetry packet confirming solar array voltage drop.
* **InSight Sol 1442 Last Transmission:** Received December 15, 2022, accompanied by the final high-gain antenna image of Elysium Planitia covered in fine Martian silt.
* **Apollo 11 Liftoff:** Quindar tones (2524 Hz tone bursts) and CAPCOM audio communication records from the NASA Johnson Space Center History Portal.
* **Surveyor 3 Inspection:** Apollo 12 Preliminary Science Report (NASA SP-235) detailing the recovery of the Surveyor 3 television camera and scoop.
